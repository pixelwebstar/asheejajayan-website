Our Philosophy

More Than Just a Cup

Velvet & Vine began with a simple idea: coffee should be an experience, not just a routine. Founded in 2024, we transformed a historic downtown building into a haven for coffee lovers.

Every cup we serve tells a story—from the high-altitude peaks of Ethiopia to the roasting drum in our back room, and finally, to your table. We believe in slow brewing, real ingredients, and the power of community.
